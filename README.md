# iTunes Scrobbler

Automatically scrobble music you play in iTunes to your enabled accounts in Scrubbler. Requires Windows and iTunes.

## Getting started

1. Open **iTunes Scrobbler** in Scrubbler and select **Connect**.
2. Play music in iTunes. Tracks scrobble after you listen to half the song or four minutes, whichever comes first.

You can enable automatic connection on startup and share what you're listening to on Discord.
